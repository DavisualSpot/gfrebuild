The liquidity gap

Keep capital working.
Keep payouts moving.

Traditional prefunding forces remittance providers to choose between locking up working capital and risking payout delays during demand spikes. GRIP changes that equation.

Without GRIPCapital sits idle while demand remains unpredictable.Large prefunded settlement balances.Shortfalls during peak transaction periods.Manual treasury management across corridors.Failed or delayed beneficiary payouts.
With GRIPLiquidity arrives precisely when a payout needs it.Reduced idle capital and prefunding pressure.Higher transaction completion reliability.Automated settlement and recovery.More efficient treasury operations.
